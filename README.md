# Aether-Drifter

**Aether-Drifter is a slow-paced spaceship survival game built from scratch using HTML Canvas and JavaScript. You spawn as a ship in the aether and must blast your way through endless waves of enemies to achieve a high score. The enemies come in multiple varieties, each with distinct movement and attack patterns. The core of the game is a dynamic risk-reward system: growing larger increases your rate of fire, but it also increases your hitbox, making you much more vulnerable to enemy attacks.**

**Controls:**

1.WASD: Navigate your ship through the infinitely generating aether.<br>
2.Left Click (Hold): Shoot projectiles.<br>
3.Spacebar: Release aether mass from your body. This shrinks your size and makes you move much faster, but decreases your shooting power.<br>
4.Shift: Dash to quickly evade enemies (has a cooldown).<br>

Survive as long as possible. The enemies will spawn in increasing numbers as the waves progress.

**Inspiration**
I wanted to build a game that combined classic arcade shooting with a dynamic physics and stats system. The idea of actively trading your size and fire rate for speed and dodging ability felt like a fresh twist on the traditional space shooter genre. I also wanted to challenge myself by building the game logic, rendering, and enemy movement patterns entirely from scratch using the HTML Canvas API. This game is inspired by some aspects of the game geometry dash as well.
<br> 
**Theme: Endless**<br>
This project fits the theme directly because the core gameplay loop revolves around shooting mechanics to survive by traversing infinite distance through the aether and facing difficult enemy waves that keep on increasing infinitely. The RPG elements are integrated through the active management of the player's stats and physical state.
