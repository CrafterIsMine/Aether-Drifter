# Aether-Drifter<br>
** A game made using canvas and javascript on the theme shooter and rpg. You will spawn in a spaceship and you have you cut your way through all the enemies to achieve a high score. The enemies also have their own varieties  and have different movement patterns. **

**Controls -**

1. Use WASD to navigate through the aether.
2. You can shoot by holding left click.
3. You can use space to release aether mass from your body and move faster but your shooting rate will decrease.
4. You can use Shift to dash which also has a cooldown.
5. Growing in size can be helpful against enemies as it increases the rate of fire but has the disadvantage of larger hitbox for enemies to attack.
6. The enemies will increase as the waves go on.
